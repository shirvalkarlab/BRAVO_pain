## Intended scientific changes

The joint optimizer adopts the latest observation-weighting policy without the earlier age penalty or fitted time term. Readiness uses evidence from a band that falls with current and rises with the selected pain outcome. Broader within-visit run selection carries the opposite side's fixed-current context. Pooled titration response is distinguished from the historical, time-confounded screening slope.

Finite nonzero point signs now determine coherence and device-direction evidence; statistical establishment is reported separately as a confidence caveat. Missing, malformed or nonfinite estimates supply no evidence. D26 inverted/too-close capture warnings use the pooled titration slope, not historical between-visit mean differences: wrong sign or exactly zero predicts the corresponding alert, while uncertainty remains explicit. These warnings do not become threshold-placement blockers. Capture artefact ceilings remain separate checks against canonical constants.

E2 stores shared biomarker AUC minus 0.5, retaining the raw AUC/interval and actual resampling unit. Exported AUC evidence retains report-level provenance even when a caller requests another raw-table cluster. Proposed plans, observed settings, statistical evidence and device constraints remain distinct outputs; the algorithms do not alter a device.
