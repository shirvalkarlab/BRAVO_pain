The notation rule, applied everywhere on this page

Contacts are printed in the form the Closed-Loop and Biomarkers pages already print (decisions 122 and 131, produced by the server's one formatter): the side as one letter, the contact numbers as digits, and each contact's polarity as a superscript. No contact number is ever spelled out as a word, and no number on the page is spelled out either: "0 of 4 arms", "12 of 18 bands", "1 of 4 checks". Every number carries its unit.
