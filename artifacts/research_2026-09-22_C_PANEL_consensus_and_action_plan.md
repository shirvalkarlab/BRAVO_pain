# Panel C — consensus and action plan on the Stim Optimizer's Bayesian algorithm

Report reviewed: `artifacts/research_2026-09-22_C_bayesian_algorithm_stim_optimizer_end_to_end.md` (its recommendation: partial overhaul, four bounded changes; no full overhaul).
Panel: three reviewers (biostatistician, engineer, pain neurologist standing in for the PI), a shared message board, discussion and voting until consensus, at most five rounds. Consensus reached in round 4 (three "yes"). Board and votes in the session scratchpad (`debate/C_board.md`, `C_*_r1..r4.md`). Measurements by the orchestrator on the live record, store writes off (`_c_design_probe.py`, `_c_two_stage_capture.py`, `_c_calibration_probe.py`, `_c_live_stratum_calibration.py`).

"P": the panel's elicited probability that the action changes or materially strengthens RCS08's programming decision (median of the final votes; range from the lowest low to the highest high of the 90% ranges); elicitation, not measurement. "Measured": the statistic on the live record.

## 1. The headline the panel agreed on

**The optimizer's engine is sound and correctly refuses to print a current, but the surrogate it draws fails its own pre-registered calibration check, and nothing on the live path runs that check.** The spec (`OBJECTIVE_SPEC.md` §6) pre-registered three criteria (`validation.PASS_CRITERION`): leave-one-epoch-out and leave-one-era-out error at most 0.90 of a precision-weighted-mean baseline, and 95% coverage 0.85-1.00 with a uniform probability-integral transform; any single failure means the surrogate may not select settings. `routines.validation` is imported nowhere on the live path and its own warm-start helper raises on the 17 hard-infeasible epochs the live objective flags, so it cannot have run since that flag was added. Run tonight: on the validation path's flat model (75 feasible of 92 epochs) all three fail by quarter (ratios 0.939 and 1.142, coverage 0.81); on the live per-rate strata the card renders, 55 Hz (60/160 µs, 19 epochs) fails all three with leave-one-quarter-out ratio 1.315, coverage 0.37 and PIT p 0.000, and 110 Hz (10 epochs) fails with 1.087. The methodologist's reading, endorsed: not mere sparsity (which gives wide, honest intervals) but a surface that moves between quarters more than the model's uncertainty allows, the non-stationarity the README names and decisions 193-196 decline to model. The clinician's reading: any current the page ever prints is not to be trusted until that instability is diagnosed, separately from the current-confound finding of panel A.

The rest of the record: 92 epochs, 64 distinct settings, 47 tried once; no rate resolves a current. The closest is the clinic stream at 55 Hz (60/160 µs, 24 epochs, 102 reports): the surface is not flat (0.97 against 0.45), the best cell beats today's by 0.74 ± 0.57, and only coverage fails, 2 pairs of the 6 required. The back site, run as the primary outcome, clears no check the left leg fails, and the clinic-stream fit ignores the requested site altogether (`clinic_pain.fit_clinic_rate_strata` hard-codes the left leg).

## 4. Where the panel disagreed, and how it resolved

- The report's first change (the boundary-avoiding kernel) was demoted by all three once the calibration failure was measured: a kernel that shapes edge variance does not fix a surface that is confidently wrong between quarters.
- The engineer corrected two small errors in the report (decision 140's 10 s is the whole first build, not Stage 1; `tests/test_surrogate.py` does not exist); the clinician corrected the report's ordering claim and refused the merge of the two readiness cards.
- Item 1 took three amendments to converge (retire now; fix the clinic-stream defect separately; defer wiring to the PI) and one more round for item 10's mechanics (the per-pair table `current_coverage` discards).
