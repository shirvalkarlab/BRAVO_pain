**How to read the uncertainty columns.** "P" is the panel's elicited probability that the action, once built, changes or materially strengthens the programming decision for RCS08 (which band, which contacts, which current, whether to deploy): the median of the three reviewers' final votes, with the range from the lowest low to the highest high of their 90% ranges. It is expert elicitation, not a measurement. "Measured" is the statistic the orchestrator computed on the live record, with its interval, where one could be computed today; those are measurements.

## 1. The headline the panel agreed on

**The pain-positive bands on the left lead rest on the current in force.** On every cell of the 21.5-26.5 Hz cluster that decision 229 called "supported" on L 1-3+ (60 s, 120-minute pre-report window, sheets off, n 59), the correlation with Left Leg VAS drops by 0.10-0.18 once the left current in force at each rating is partialled out (`stats_utils.partial_corr`), and every adjusted interval spans zero; the drop's block-bootstrap interval excludes zero in all six cells (24.5 Hz: r 0.331, 0.17-0.49, to 0.170, -0.05 to 0.39; drop -0.161, -0.31 to -0.04, shuffle p 0.030). Pain and power both fall with the current (r -0.40 and -0.50): the shared-cause pattern the audit predicted. The same holds, more weakly, for the ladder's own watch bands on L 0-3+ under the readiness screen's settings (NRS, 60-minute window): 24.5, 25.5, 26.5 and 27.5 Hz all lose their wholly-positive interval (24.5 Hz 0.207 to 0.113, -0.07 to 0.27; 27.5 Hz 0.213 to 0.175, -0.01 to 0.34); NRS falls with the left current at r -0.45. The heat-map grid computes a plain Pearson r with no term for the current (`analytics._weighted_pearson_matrix`); the older per-session search has the adjustment (`pipeline.py`, `partial_corr`) and the grid does not.

What this means for the next visit (the clinician's reading, endorsed by the panel): the 24.5-27.5 Hz bands are not a pre-specified watch list for the biomarker's pain relationship on the ladder's ramp (part A); a band earns credit only if it also moves during the fixed-current holds (part B). The ramp still measures the current-to-power slope, which is what part A is for.

3. Given §1, whether the L 0-3+ 125 Hz cell should stay the readiness screen's chosen cell for the exploratory ladder: its four pain-positive bands survive as point signs but none clears zero once the current is partialled out. The ladder's part A (current-to-power) is unaffected; part B (acute pain) is where those bands would earn or lose credit.

## 4. Where the panel disagreed, and how it resolved

- Cost of item 1: "low, one term per cell" (report) against 2-3 days (engineer: the grid's matcher carries no per-sample current, the store's rule version must bump or every stored grid keeps serving the old numbers, six test files construct the call). The panel took the engineer's figure.
- Source of the current for item 1: the orchestrator's probe used the Stim Optimizer's epoch table; the engineer showed Biomarkers may not import StimOptimizer (a tested rule) and named the in-module source. Adopted in round 4.
- Item 2: the methodologist argued a global-shuffle test does not need independent settings (correct) but, once the proxy was measured, that the proxy answers the question for today's record; the engineer had costed the naive re-run at 110-220 h. Scoped down to the record.
- Item 8: two reviewers withheld consensus until the PI's ruling was recorded as an open question rather than decided by the panel; the protocol allows that and they accepted it in round 4.
