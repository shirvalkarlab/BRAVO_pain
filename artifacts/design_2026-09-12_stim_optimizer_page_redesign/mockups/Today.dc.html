A cell is deployable only if a majority of scanned bands respond, the slope survives era blocking, and the amplitude contrast sits at or below the flat 5.0 mA hard limit. That limit is PI-declared and was established by testing at 165 Hz; it does not vary with rate or pulse width. An earlier version of this panel applied an energy-matched ceiling that scaled as the square root of 55/f — that model has been withdrawn, because tolerable amplitude at a given frequency is not governed by total delivered energy. The amplitude condition still matters for the same reason it always did: a response measured only above the amplitude we are willing to program was never deployable evidence. (110 words)
