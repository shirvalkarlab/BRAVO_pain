# Panel B — consensus and action plan on alternative biomarker-detection and symptom-tracking methods

Report reviewed: `artifacts/research_2026-09-22_B_alternative_detection_and_symptom_tracking.md`.
Panel: three reviewers (biostatistician, engineer, pain neurologist standing in for the PI), a shared message board, discussion and voting until consensus, at most five rounds. Consensus reached in round 3 (three "yes"). The board with every message and vote is in the session scratchpad (`debate/B_board.md`, `B_*_r1..r3.md`). The reviewers' own pre-build tests were run by the orchestrator before round 2 on the live record (store writes off; the rating-only model on the host's statsmodels).

"P" is the panel's elicited probability that the action changes or materially strengthens RCS08's programming decision (median of the three final votes; range from the lowest low to the highest high of the 90% ranges); it is elicitation, not a measurement. "Measured" is the statistic computed on the live record with its interval.

## 1. The headline the panel agreed on

**Of the report's thirteen methods, one is a build now, three are guards, and the rest are either not identifiable on this record or wait on a gate.** The one build is not new machinery: it is presenting decision 230's three fixed-current holds (off / on / off, five minutes each, a rating a minute, the patient blind to the current) as the titration card's first pain reading after the visit, because that is the only stream in the record where pain is measured with the current held, and the current in force is now known to be a shared cause of band power and pain on the left lead (panel A). The two headline proposals of the report fail their own pre-build tests: a two-state model of the ratings cannot recover its dwell times from data simulated under its own fit, and invents a larger regime gap on pure noise than it finds in the real series; and the multivariate decoder cannot be justified until the current-partialled all-bands diagnostic clears chance and a device-allowed sensing pair carries a pain-positive band (today: none).

Stops and gates (adopted as such):
- **4. The HMM / smoothed-label hybrid: do not build; record the identifiability result in the decision log.** Measured: Left Leg VAS, 513 ratings, lag-1 autocorrelation 0.42; a two-regime Markov-switching fit gives means 49.4 and 71.4, dwell 11.3 and 11.7 ratings (AIC 4,188 against 4,329 for one state), but on 60 series simulated from that fit only 29 refits converge and they recover dwell times of 1.0 (1.0-1.3) and 220 (56-405) ratings; on one-state noise of the same length the two-state fit invents a 45-point (35-58) regime gap against the real 22. P that building it would have been wasted or misleading: 90% (70-99), the methodologist's framing; the engineer and clinician gave 5% (0-15) that recording it changes a decision.
- **5. The multivariate decoder for band selection: diagnostic first, build gated.** Run item 2's all-bands diagnostic with item 7's embargo, reported against the current-alone floor and the shuffle null (item 9 folded in). The 20-28 h detached-job build (`_spawn_detached` + marker file, the stability-precompute pattern; time-blocked CV; `auc_block_perm_null`) proceeds only if BOTH the current-partialled all-bands AUC clears the null's 95th percentile by a margin stated before the run AND a sensing pair the device allows under decision 217 carries a pain-positive band on the plain grid. Otherwise stop after the diagnostic and log the numbers. P that the full build ever proceeds and changes the decision: 12% (1-45).

Dropped by all three without a vote: the aperiodic / specparam rebuild (decision 206 stands; `specparam` is still pinned in `requirements.txt`, a housekeeping note), coherence between the leads, dynamic time warping, the Kalman latent-pain-state filter.

## 4. Where the panel disagreed, and how it resolved

- The report's two top recommendations (label smoothing, decoder) were ranked first by the report and last by the clinician; the pre-build tests settled it: one fails identifiability, the other waits on a gate all three wrote in nearly the same words.
- The engineer re-costed the HMM build from the report's "medium" to 24-32 h (no HMM library in the container; a new label metric, a rule-version bump, a two-heat-map comparison); moot once the identifiability test failed.
- The pooled table "lower pain at higher current" was read by the clinician as "the good visits were the high-current visits", not as a dosing signal, because the current does not forecast the pain label out of sample; that reading became item 8.
